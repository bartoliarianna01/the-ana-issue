HOW TO ADD YOUR VIDEO TO PAGE 9

1. Export your video as an MP4 file (H.264 format works best).
2. Rename it exactly: friendship-video.mp4
3. Copy it into this folder, next to index.html.
4. Double-click index.html and navigate to page 9.
5. Press play.

IMPORTANT: When uploading your website to GitHub, also upload friendship-video.mp4.
If the video is larger than 100 MB, compress it first.
